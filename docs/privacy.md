# Privacy notice

Poroleague is a community league run by volunteer organizers of the Porobound Discord server. This page says what the site stores about you, why, and what you can ask for.

## Who to contact

The league organizers. Message any organizer on the Porobound Discord server.

## What we store

When you log in with Discord:

- your Discord user id, your server nickname (or Discord name) and your avatar;
- a Discord login token, so the site can check you're still in the server and whether you're an organizer. It can only read your profile and your membership of the server.

When you play:

- your sign-ups, match results, the legends you played and who won the dice roll;
- the reason you give when you flag a result;
- a log of reports, flags and organizer changes, with who made them.

## Why

To run the league: sign-ups, schedules, standings and settling disputes. This is our legitimate interest in running the league you signed up for. Results, standings and the change log are public on the site, so every player can check them.

## Discord posts

The site posts results and pairings in the league's Discord channels, naming the players. These posts are removed when the season finishes.

## How long we keep it

- Logins expire after at most 7 days.
- Finished seasons stay on the site as league history.

## Your rights

- **See your data:** ask an organizer and we'll send you what the site stores about you.
- **Be erased:** ask an organizer. We anonymise you: your name becomes "Former player" with a short random code, and your avatar, Discord link, logins and flag reasons are removed. Your results in finished seasons stay, so those standings don't change. In a season that's still running, you leave it: off the signup list or groups if they aren't locked yet, or dropped once play has started, which voids your matches in that season (as when any player drops out). If you ask during a season, we also remove Discord posts that name you within one month.
- You can also complain to your national data protection authority.

## Backups

The database is backed up every night. The 14 most recent backups are kept on the server, and encrypted copies are kept off the server for 30 days. Backups are only used to recover from a failure. Erased data stays in backups until they're replaced, and if we ever restore one, we erase the same players again.

## Discord

You log in with Discord, and Discord has its own privacy policy. Messages you send on Discord itself are outside this site.
