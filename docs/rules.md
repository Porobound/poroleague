# Poroleague Rules

Items marked **[TBD]** are not decided yet.

## 1. Who can play

- Any member of the Porobound Discord server can play. Log in to the league site with your Discord account.
- Sign up on the site while signup is open. You can withdraw until signup closes.
- Organizers are the members with the organizer role in Discord. Organizers can play too.
- The league is free to enter. There is no buy-in.

## 2. Season format

- **Groups.** A group has at most 16 players. With up to 16 the season has one group; with more, players are drawn at random into as few groups as fit, up to 8. Groups can be uneven by one (17 players: 9 and 8; 40 players: 14, 13 and 13). A season takes at most 128 players; once it's full, a spot opens only if someone withdraws.
- **Round robin.** You play every other player in your group once. In a group of 12 that's 11 matches.
- **Byes.** If a group has an odd number of players, one player sits out each round. A bye is not a match and gives no points.
- **Schedule.** The schedule is set when groups are locked. Each week has a set number of rounds, shown on the season page. With 2 rounds a week, a 12-player group runs about 6 weeks.
- **Season deadline.** Every season has a hard deadline at 23:59 Helsinki time (Europe/Helsinki) on the deadline date.
- **Top cut.** After the group stage, the top players go on to a single-elimination top cut. With one group, it's seeded by the standings: 1st plays the last qualifier, 2nd plays the second-to-last, and so on. With two groups, it's cross-seeded: group A's 1st plays group B's last qualifier, group B's 1st plays group A's last, and so on. With more groups, seeding is set with the top cut. The top players of each group qualify; how many is set per season and shown on the season page. When the top cut is played, and prizes: **[TBD]**.

## 3. Playing a match

- Matches are best of three (Bo3) games of Riftbound. Play online or in person, whichever you and your opponent prefer.
- Contact your opponent on Discord and agree when and where to play. Aim to play during the week the match is scheduled.
- **Agree the terms first.** Before the match begins, agree with your opponent how much time you have for it, so nobody ends up in a much longer match than they planned for. Stick to what you agreed.
- **Dice roll.** Roll a die before the match, as in the official Riftbound rules. The winner's name is recorded with the result.
- **Legends.** Each player plays one legend for the whole match. You can switch legends between matches.
- **Decks.** No decklists. Play any legal deck you like, and change it between matches, but not between games.
- **Draws.** A match that ends level (1-1 or 0-0), including when your agreed time runs out, is a draw. A single game can also end in a draw.
- **Ending early.** If the match ends at 1-0, because your agreed time runs out or you both agree to stop, the player ahead wins the match.
- Game rules, deck construction and banned cards follow the official Riftbound rules.

## 4. Points and standings

- Win 3 points, draw 1 point, loss 0 points.
- Standings are ordered by points. Ties are broken in this order:
  1. Head-to-head: points from matches between the tied players only.
  2. Game-win %: games won ÷ games played.
  3. If still tied, the organizers decide the order.

## 5. Reporting results

- After the match, either player reports it on the site: who won each game (or Draw), both legends and who won the dice roll.
- **Games.** Game 1 is required; report games 2 and 3 only if you played them. Games are in order, with no gaps, and the match ends once a player has won 2 games, so there is no game 3 after a 2-0.
- The result counts as soon as it's reported, and it's posted in Discord.
- The site asks you to confirm before saving. **A saved report is final:** neither player can edit it.
- **Flags.** If a result is wrong, either player flags it and gives a reason. This includes the reporter fixing their own mistake. After a flag, only organizers can change the result. They either correct it or keep it as reported. A result can only be flagged once. You can still flag after the season deadline, until the season is finalized.
- Every report, flag and organizer decision is listed in the public audit log.

## 6. Late and unplayed matches

- A match not played in its week is **overdue**. Overdue matches can still be played and reported normally until the season deadline.
- Reporting closes at the season deadline. A match still unplayed then counts as a loss for both players (0 points each), unless an organizer enters a result.
- When organizers enter a result for an unplayed match (e.g. one player didn't show up): **[TBD]**.

## 7. Dropping out

- If you can't finish the season, tell an organizer.
- When you drop, **all** your matches in that season, played and unplayed, are void. They don't count for anyone's standings. Your name stays in the group, marked as dropped.
- Dropping is final: you can't rejoin the same season.
- Being erased from the site (see the [privacy notice](/privacy)) during a running season counts as dropping.

## 8. Organizers

- Organizers run the season: they open and close signup, draw the groups, resolve flags, handle drops and break ties.
- Organizers can edit any result. Every change is in the public audit log, including changes to their own matches.
- Organizer decisions on disputes are final.

## 9. Conduct

- Be on time, be honest about results and be decent to your opponent.
- Riftbound's official code of conduct applies.

## Ideas for future seasons

Not rules yet. These may change how later seasons work, and will be decided before any season uses them.

- **Seeded groups instead of random ones.** When there are two or more groups, they could be filled based on how players did in earlier seasons, rather than drawn at random.
- **Divisions.** The groups could become ranked divisions (e.g. Division 1 and Division 2). At the end of a season, the top players of a lower division move up and the bottom players of a higher division move down. How many players move, and how new players are placed, would be decided first.
