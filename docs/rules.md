# Poroleague Rules

Draft v0.1. Items marked **[TBD]** are not decided yet.

## 1. Who can play

- Any member of the Porobound Discord server can play. Log in to the league site with your Discord account.
- Sign up on the site while signup is open. You can withdraw until signup closes.
- Organizers are the members with the organizer role in Discord. Organizers can play too.
- The league is free to enter. There is no buy-in.

## 2. Season format

- **Groups.** With fewer than 16 players the season has one group. With 16 or more, players are drawn at random into two groups (they can be uneven, e.g. 13 and 12).
- **Round robin.** You play every other player in your group once. In a group of 12 that's 11 matches.
- **Byes.** If a group has an odd number of players, one player sits out each round. A bye is not a match and gives no points.
- **Schedule.** The schedule is set when groups are locked. Each week has 2 rounds, so a 12-player group runs about 6 weeks.
- **Season deadline.** Every season has a hard deadline at 23:59 Helsinki time (Europe/Helsinki) on the deadline date.
- **Top cut.** After the group stage, the top players go on to a single-elimination top cut. With one group, it's seeded by the standings: 1st plays the last qualifier, 2nd plays the second-to-last, and so on. With two groups, it's cross-seeded: group A's 1st plays group B's last qualifier, group B's 1st plays group A's last, and so on. How many qualify, when the top cut is played, and prizes: **[TBD]**.

## 3. Playing a match

- Matches are best of three (Bo3) games of Riftbound. Play online or in person, whichever you and your opponent prefer.
- Contact your opponent on Discord and agree when and where to play. Aim to play during the week the match is scheduled.
- **Dice roll.** Roll a die before the match, as in the official Riftbound rules. The winner's name is recorded with the result.
- **Legends.** Each player plays one legend for the whole match. You can switch legends between matches.
- **Decks.** No decklists. Play any legal deck you like, and change it between matches, but not between games.
- **Draws.** If the match ends 1-1, it is a draw.
- Game rules, deck construction and banned cards follow the official Riftbound rules.

## 4. Points and standings

- Win 3 points, draw 1 point, loss 0 points.
- Standings are ordered by points. Ties are broken in this order:
  1. Head-to-head: points from matches between the tied players only.
  2. Game-win %: games won ÷ games played.
  3. If still tied, the organizers decide the order.

## 5. Reporting results

- After the match, either player reports it on the site: the score, both legends and who won the dice roll.
- The result counts as soon as it's reported, and it's posted in Discord.
- The reporter can edit their report until the opponent flags it.
- **Flags.** If the report is wrong, the opponent flags it and gives a reason. After a flag, only organizers can change the result. They either correct it or keep it as reported. A result can only be flagged once.
- Every report, edit, flag and organizer decision is listed in the public audit log.

## 6. Late and unplayed matches

- A match not played in its week is **overdue**. Overdue matches can still be played and reported normally until the season deadline.
- Reporting closes at the season deadline. A match still unplayed then counts as a loss for both players (0 points each), unless an organizer enters a result.

## 7. Dropping out

- If you can't finish the season, tell an organizer.
- When you drop, **all** your matches in that season, played and unplayed, are void. They don't count for anyone's standings. Your name stays in the group, marked as dropped.
- Whether a dropped player can rejoin the same season: **[TBD]**.

## 8. Organizers

- Organizers run the season: they open and close signup, draw the groups, resolve flags, handle drops and break ties.
- Organizers can edit any result. Every change is in the public audit log, including changes to their own matches.
- Organizer decisions on disputes are final.

- Be on time, be honest about results and be decent to your opponent.
